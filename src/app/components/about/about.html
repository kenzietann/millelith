About

Small on purpose

Millelith started in Osaka as a three-person team of engineers who were tired of choosing between shipping fast and shipping safely. We still work that way — small, hands-on, and close to the code.

We're not a 200-person agency handing your project to whoever's free. Every engagement is staffed by people who write the code and run the exploits themselves — no bench, no hand-offs, no account managers relaying messages on your behalf.
